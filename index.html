    A small, open experiment in the agentic web.
    Made for curious people, not perfect predictions.

    For agents & builders

    Use window.AgenticWeb.load() then run(goal). Same-origin windows can send {type:"agentic-web:invoke", goal, id} and receive {type:"agentic-web:result", id, result}. Cross-origin messaging is denied.

    Auto-run: ?goal=…; machine output: ?goal=…&format=json.
